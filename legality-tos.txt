  Good question - you should know the risks:

  LinkedIn's Terms of Service

  Technically, no. LinkedIn's https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement prohibits:
  - Using bots, scrapers, or automated tools to access their services
  - Copying data obtained through their platform without permission

  This extension extracts data from the DOM, which falls under "automated data collection."

  Practical Reality

  Potential Consequences

  - LinkedIn could restrict or ban your account if they detect automation
  - Detection is unlikely for occasional manual use (you click a button on profiles you're viewing)
  - More likely to be flagged if you summarize hundreds of profiles rapidly

  The Gray Area

  The 2022 hiQ Labs v. LinkedIn ruling established that scraping public data isn't necessarily
  illegal, but:
  - You're accessing as a logged-in user (different rules)
  - ToS violation ≠ criminal, but LinkedIn can enforce it

  My Recommendation

  Use at your own discretion. If you're concerned:
  - Use the Boolean Search Builder and Question Generator (no scraping involved)
  - Manually copy/paste profile info instead of auto-extracting
  - Consider official tools like LinkedIn Recruiter

  It's your call whether the convenience is worth the (small) risk.
